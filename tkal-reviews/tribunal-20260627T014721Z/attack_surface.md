# Pre-disclosed weak points (examiners attack these first, then hunt for undisclosed gaps)

Each is tagged to the criterion it most threatens.

1. **[claim_strength] W1 goalpost.** The pre-registered target was ½tr(RIV) ≈ 2.77. The pooled estimate is 2.43 ± 0.26 — about 0.34 *below* 2.77, i.e. the registered point target sits at the very edge of / outside the ±0.26 interval. The paper then "refines" the target to a band 2.42–2.55 "once the missing-at-random term was derived," and the estimate lands inside the refined band. Is the augmentation a principled, independently-derived O(1) MAR correction, or post-hoc goalpost-moving that converts a near-miss of the registered target into a pass? Is "confirms the completed theorem" earned, given the registered target was 2.77?

2. **[mechanism_not_artifact] The "100% land on saturated" signature.** `W3C_high_RIV_frac = 1.000` in every cell. The candidate set is *nested* and ordered by missing information, with the saturated model at the top. The deviance bias is monotone in tr(RIV). So any downward-biased deviance most helps the highest-RIV candidate by construction. Is "all errors land on the saturated candidate" a substantive empirical confirmation of the mechanism, or a near-tautology of how the candidate set is ordered? Would any monotone downward bias produce the same 1.000 regardless of whether the paper's specific decomposition is correct?

3. **[mechanism_not_artifact / claim_strength] "1.000 even under uncongeniality."** The 1.000 fraction holds in uncongenial cells too, but there the misclassification count collapses (~130 vs ~660), the uncorrected rate *exceeds* the oracle, and the correction *overshoots* (oracle − corrected goes negative). Does the directional signature carry any evidential weight in the uncongenial regime, where the sign of the problem reverses? Is reporting the signature only for congenial cells the right scope, or does the uncongenial behavior undercut the headline mechanism?

4. **[generic_not_lucky] Block-diagonal equality.** The projected-trace formula's advantage over the naive trace difference is the paper's claimed contribution for the LRT/IC correction. In the ladder's candidate family, naive and projected traces agree to 3.6×10⁻¹⁵ — the formulas are numerically identical there. The 0.080 selection-rate gap (0.824 vs 0.904) that distinguishes them comes from the *frozen-SB / naive-input* arm, not from a cell where the two trace formulas themselves differ. Does Study 7 actually demonstrate the projected formula's superiority, or only demonstrate it in a family where it provably cannot differ, with the "off-equality cost" carried by a different comparison? Is the generality claim ("overstates the projected trace elsewhere") earned by these data or imported from the derivation?

5. **[mechanism_not_artifact] The 52-σ discrimination.** Study 4 rejects the naive formula at 52 standard errors by engineering one coordinate to 70–79% missing and another to ~0%. Is a 52-σ separation evidence that the projected formula is *correct*, or only that the design was built to maximally separate two formulas that the derivation already says differ? Does it speak to realistic missingness, or is its only function to exclude a strawman? What would the discrimination be at a realistic, balanced missingness rate — and is that reported?

6. **[claim_strength] "Statistically indistinguishable" / "calibrates completely."** Study 7 calls 0.904/0.903 vs oracle 0.899 "statistically indistinguishable" at MC SE ≈ 0.010. The two-moment/equating arms slightly *exceed* the oracle. Is "reaches the oracle" the right claim, or is there a small systematic overshoot being read as noise? Separately, the weak-signal floor (no arm > 0.650, oracle itself 0.820) is attributed to "information loss rather than calibration error" — is that attribution demonstrated by the internals, or asserted?

7. **[claim_strength] Disclosed failures — are they fully disclosed?** Two failures are in the main text (W1 N=1000 at −2.6 SE; W4 similar-pair below resolution, pilot −2 dissolved). Are there *other* unmet pre-registered predictions or arms that quietly did not pass and are reported only as totals? The MNAR cells of the selection sweep, for instance, are not in the S5 headline — were they pre-registered to pass, and if so where is their grade?

8. **[reproducible_numbers] RDS-backed headlines.** Studies 1–5 are backed by `.rds` files not transcribed in the appendix (only Studies 6–8's CSVs are). The panel cannot independently recompute 2.43 ± 0.26, 0.042/0.034, or 2.64/2.70 from the inlined data. Treat those as asserted-from-artifact and judge internal consistency and the goalpost/scope questions; flag any that cannot be checked even in principle.
