## Summary verdict

The manuscript's *architecture* already matches the discipline: roadmaps, the organizing
question, numbered contributions, theorem/proof rhythm, candid failed-prediction
reporting, and explicit limitation paragraphs are all register-native (R7–R9). What does
not match is the *sentence texture* and part of the *vocabulary*: the prose is built from
short unsubordinated declaratives (15.2 words/sentence against an exemplar band of
17.4–26.4; 0.72 commas/sentence against 0.99–3.39; parentheses at one tenth register
density), it opens 36% of sentences with "The" (register: 14%), it repeats a small set of
house formulas where the register varies, and it uses a conversational vocabulary layer
(sits/lands/floor/headline/junk/genuinely/scoped) that no paper in the owned corpus uses.
Marcus's diagnosis is confirmed on both counts, and the cause is traceable to specific
house rules (C1–C8 in the rubric), not to drafting accident.
